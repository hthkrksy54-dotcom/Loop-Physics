ytauto – Quiet Thoughts & Loop Physics

ytauto is a private tool of one creator. It produces original videos – calm video essays about philosophy and psychology (Quiet Thoughts) and generated, oddly satisfying physics simulations (Loop Physics) – and shares them to the creator's own accounts on YouTube, Instagram and TikTok through their official APIs.

Every video is made from scratch in code: the physics, the picture and the sound (public-domain melodies). No third-party content is used, and no data about other users is collected.
